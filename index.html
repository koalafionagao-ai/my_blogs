Payment_Uber: Fintech From Platform Infra to a New Traffic Gateway

The Marketplace economic model and vast global expansion dictate Uber's complex fund flows and data links. Dealing with massive transaction volumes while consistently performing multi-role, localized settlements in different regions inevitably brings up the challenge of managing currencies and FX rates across the entire transaction cycle. Uber’s in-house **Real-time Ledger** system and **Hybrid Reconciliation** mechanism provide a blueprint for how a multinational platform can steadily expand both its business lines and its market reach.
